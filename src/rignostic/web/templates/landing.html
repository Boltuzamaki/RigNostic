Why this exists

Facial rigs often fail only after animation starts.

A rig can look perfect in a neutral pose while hiding broken drivers, incorrect limits, swapped controls, or deformation that appears only at high values or in combinations.

RigNostic turns that manual QA loop into an inspect → test → diagnose workflow.

Test selection

A failed control determines the next check.

RigNostic requests another Blender inspection when a control response looks wrong. Low-confidence findings are left for review.

  • Prioritize suspicious controls
  • Select the next inspection tool
  • Compare Blender data and renders
